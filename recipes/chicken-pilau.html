Chicken Pilau

Description

Chicken pilau is a popular East African rice dish cooked with chicken, onions, and aromatic spices.

It is flavourful and can be served for lunch, dinner, or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 500 g of chicken
  • 2 onions
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon of pilau masala
  • 2 tablespoons of cooking oil
  • 4 cups of water or stock, adjusted as needed
  • Salt to taste

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 500 g of chicken
  • 2 onions
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon of pilau masala
  • 2 tablespoons of cooking oil
  • 4 cups of water or stock, adjusted as needed
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Wash the rice and set it aside.
  2. Heat oil in a cooking pot.
  3. Fry the onions until golden brown.
  4. Add garlic, pilau masala, and chicken.
  5. Add the rice, water or stock, and salt.
  6. Cover and cook until the rice is tender and the chicken is fully cooked.
  7. Serve hot.
